What We Do

A complete real-estate & construction partner

Eight services under one trusted name — from the first survey to the final paint coat, and from buying your plot to handing over the keys.

Construction

Residential and commercial builds — RCC, structural, finishing — engineered to local soil & seismic conditions.

Renovation

Selective demolition, structural retrofits and full home makeovers that respect the original character of the property.

Sale & Purchase

Plot, flat and house transactions across Dehradun — title checks, paperwork and registration handled end-to-end.

Interior Designing

Functional, livable interiors — modular kitchens, wardrobes, false-ceilings and lighting — tailored to your budget.

Estimate & Survey

Property Dealing

Building Supervision

General Suppliers

Cement, steel, bricks, sand, aggregates and finishing materials — supplied at fair, transparent rates.
